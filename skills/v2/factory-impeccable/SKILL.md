Adapted from the local Impeccable 4.8.0 workflow and craft floor. The supplied client direction and fidelity axes replace an interactive interview in this unattended factory. Choose surface purpose per route: persuade, operate, read or experience. Product shopping and checkout must remain clear, even when a campaign surface is expressive.

Ground the visual world in the actual client, products and verified assets. On free axes, make an intentional choice of composition, typography, color, imagery and pacing before implementation; avoid a canned category template. On matched axes preserve the reference. Do not invent factual copy or substitute decorative CSS for missing product photography.

In one batched look at real renders, inspect hierarchy, content wrapping, spacing groups, text contrast, image crops, keyboard focus and interactive states. Check the actual computed values at mobile, desktop, short landscape and narrow stress widths. Repair all findings from that look before capturing again. Body and placeholder contrast must reach 4.5:1; large type needs 3:1. Reduced motion must remain usable. Loading, empty, disabled and error states should explain the next action.

When axes are free, challenge interchangeable hero/stat/card scaffolds, unrelated decorative gradients, glass panels, stock technical monospace, repeated reveal animations and ornamental labels. Every component should serve the content. Use a coherent real icon system or authored geometric SVG; do not create fake illustrative scenes or make claims through badges. Respect confirmed reference styling where it is part of the copy requirement.

Use only the controlled factory tools. Keep a concrete design record and inspect the rendered result before publishing a candidate. A builder's self-review supplements the independent reviewer; it does not approve release.
