The supplied brief wins. Work within its confirmed identity, content, language and reference mode. Make routine choices autonomously and record assumptions; do not insert approval rounds into this factory workflow. Missing client facts/assets remain unresolved inputs rather than fabricated claims.

Feature scope comes from the client prompt, feature list and required acceptance cases. A reference journey is evidence to understand, not an automatic request to add that feature. Keep unrequested reference forms or integrations as observations/unknowns; do not introduce their panels, dependencies or delivery requirements into the client build.

Give the site a concrete point of view grounded in its products, audience and actual reference evidence. Explain what a visitor should understand or do in the first viewport. Assign a surface mode to each page: persuade, operate, read or experience. A campaign can persuade while checkout operates; expressive decoration must not obstruct product tasks.

Choose a coherent visual grammar: type voices and scale, purposeful color roles, spacing rhythm, composition, imagery, borders/radii and restrained motion. Vary layout where content calls for it. Avoid default combinations of centered oversized claims, gradient blobs, glass panels, repeated icon tiles and interchangeable rounded cards unless the brief/reference specifically calls for them. Familiar commerce patterns earn their place through clarity; originality does not require making checkout unfamiliar.

Use client assets and verified available fonts. Preserve Persian/RTL legibility and mixed numerals/Latin text. Describe how real product photography and long product titles fit; decorative CSS is not a substitute for missing merchandise imagery. Do not invent testimonials, customer counts, certifications, discounts or urgency. Draft content is labelled and blocks final delivery until resolved.

For inspiration, name the reference patterns retained and the deliberate changes for this client. For replica, prioritize measured geometry, typography, image treatment and observed states at matching sizes; document every necessary departure and unresolved fidelity dependency instead of silently redesigning.

Specify mobile navigation, hierarchy changes, content wrapping, image crops and sticky behavior. Include narrow, short landscape and wide stress sizes, keyboard focus, empty/error/loading states and reduced motion. Keep body text readable and controls usable rather than hiding overflow or shrinking the entire layout.

When `factory_design` is available, submit the structured design through it. Include actual reference evidence IDs, tokens, every required page, client-content/asset gaps and an implementation/check plan tied to the protected requirements. The controller produces DESIGN.md from the validated record. A valid plan is not proof of visual quality; the later independent reviewer judges rendered work. Builders follow the assigned design record and verify actual previews before publishing candidates.
