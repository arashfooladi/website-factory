# Feature reference

## Full-site editing

- Block-based header, footer, checkout header and checkout footer.
- Editable templates for pages, posts, archives, search, 404 and coming-soon pages.
- Custom full-width and landing-page templates.
- Global colors, typography, spacing, radii, shadows and layout widths through `theme.json`.
- Emerald, midnight, warm and purple style variations.

## WooCommerce

- Product catalog with filters, categories, active filters, sorting, results count and pagination.
- Dedicated product category, tag and attribute templates.
- Product search result template.
- Product page with gallery, title, rating, price, short description, add-to-cart form, metadata, reassurance, details and related products.
- Wide Cart, Checkout, My Account and Order Confirmation layouts.
- Stable theme-owned `maintheme-product-card` classes added to product cards at render time.
- Responsive cards, catalog sidebar, checkout summary and account navigation.

## Iran commerce companion plugin

- Iran-only allowed and shipping countries by default.
- Guest country defaults to Iran without preselecting a province.
- Required province, city, detailed address, postcode and phone fields.
- Persian and Arabic digit normalization.
- `+98`, `0098`, `98` and `9xxxxxxxxx` mobile normalization to `09xxxxxxxxx`.
- 11-digit Iranian mobile validation.
- 10-digit postcode validation for billing and shipping.
- Classic Checkout and Checkout Block support.
- Persian labels for standard shipping methods.
- Does not disable shipping calculation, force stock management, force backorders or choose a payment gateway.

## Admin setup

- Theme status dashboard.
- Explicit non-destructive starter-page installer.
- Announcement, contact, social, footer and product reassurance settings.
- Direct links to Site Editor, Global Styles, patterns, pages, products and WooCommerce settings.

## Patterns

The theme includes hero, categories, sales, promotions, arrivals, best sellers, features, testimonials, FAQ, newsletter, brand strip, blog posts, contact call-to-action, product reassurance and empty-state patterns.
