# WooCommerce responsibilities

## The theme controls

- Visual layout and spacing.
- Product card presentation.
- Product archive and single-product templates.
- Cart, checkout, account and confirmation wrappers.
- Persian labels written directly in bundled templates and patterns.

## The companion plugin controls

- Iran-only country rules.
- Mobile and postcode normalization and validation.
- Address-field requirements.
- Standard shipping-label localization.

## WooCommerce or dedicated plugins control

- Currency and Rial/Toman conversion.
- Official bank gateways.
- Tax and invoice rules.
- Shipping zones, rate calculation and carrier selection.
- Stock management and backorders.
- SMS notifications.
- Product-specific legal or medical rules.
- Order fulfillment and accounting.

This separation prevents a visual theme update or theme switch from changing historical order behavior.
